# App-review notes

## Reviewer access

No account, credentials, subscription, purchase, or special hardware is required to review the jar builder.

To exercise the flow:

1. Enter a valid public Solana or Base receiving address. No private key or seed phrase is ever requested.
2. Optionally enter a display name and message.
3. Tap Save jar.
4. Review the generated QR code and use Copy link or Share.
5. Tap Open payment page to open the hosted supporter flow in the system browser.

Reviewers do not need to complete a blockchain transaction. Wallet approval is outside the app and should be cancelled unless the reviewer intentionally wishes to send funds.

## Business model and payments

Propz is a free, non-custodial tip-link utility. It does not sell digital content, subscriptions, app features, tokens, NFTs, investments, exchange services, or wallet custody.

A tip is a completely optional monetary gift from one individual to another and is not connected to receiving digital content or services. A disclosed 1% platform fee is added on top of the supporter’s chosen tip amount; 100% of the chosen tip amount goes to the receiving address, subject to network behavior and the supporter’s wallet approval. The recipient does not pay the platform fee from the chosen gift amount.

Propz does not store cryptocurrency, execute an exchange, mine cryptocurrency, promise returns, or reward users for completing tasks. The user supplies only public receiving addresses. Transfers are initiated and approved by the supporter in a compatible external wallet.

## Apple policy context

- Guideline 3.1.1: no app feature or digital content is unlocked by a tip.
- Guideline 3.2.1(vii): the gift is optional, and the chosen gift amount goes in full to the receiver; the disclosed platform fee is added separately.
- Guideline 3.1.5: Propz is not a wallet, exchange, mining app, ICO, or crypto-securities product.

If App Review interprets the separate platform fee as inconsistent with 3.2.1(vii), do not mischaracterize the product. Pause distribution and obtain a policy/legal decision about an iOS-specific fee model or removing the payment call-to-action.

## Google Play policy context

The app should complete the Financial features declaration. Explain that it creates links for voluntary non-custodial person-to-person transfers; it does not provide a wallet, exchange, token sale, rewards, lending, or custody. The native app requests no high-risk Android permissions.
