# Store screenshot plan

Capture production or preview builds, not Expo Go. Do not include real balances, seed phrases, private keys, personal notifications, or an address that the company does not control.

## Phone set

1. **Create your tip jar** — clean initial form with a company-controlled test receiving address and `Saylor Innovations` display name.
2. **Make it yours** — name, message, and accent controls visible.
3. **Your QR is ready** — generated QR code and “tip jar is ready” heading.
4. **Share anywhere** — Copy link and Share actions visible.
5. **Non-custodial by design** — disclosure and privacy link visible; caption: `Your wallet stays yours.`

Use the same truthful UI on both stores. Suggested marketing captions may be placed outside the device frame, but never cover controls or imply unsupported functionality.

## Required source captures

- iPhone 6.9-inch display class portrait.
- iPad 13-inch display class portrait because `supportsTablet` is enabled. If tablet presentation is not polished, disable iPad support before building instead of submitting stretched phone screenshots.
- Android phone portrait at 1080 × 1920 or another Play-accepted 16:9/9:16 size.

## Graphic assets

- App Store icon: `assets/icon.png` (1024 × 1024, opaque full square).
- Play Store high-resolution icon: export `assets/icon.png` at 512 × 512.
- Play feature graphic: create a 1024 × 500 brand graphic after final in-app screenshots are captured.
