### Description

Propz makes it simple to create and share a cryptocurrency tip jar without giving up control of your wallet.

Add a public Solana or Base receiving address, personalize your jar, and generate a shareable link and QR code. Supporters open your hosted payment page and approve any transfer in their own compatible wallet.

Features:

- Accept tips to a public Solana or Base address
- Support SOL and USDC payment flows
- Create a reusable QR code and link
- Add your name, message, and accent color
- Save jar settings locally on your device
- Copy or share your jar in a few taps

Propz is non-custodial. It never asks for a private key or seed phrase and never holds funds. Blockchain addresses and transactions are public. A disclosed 1% platform fee is added to a supporter’s chosen tip amount, so the recipient receives 100% of that chosen tip amount, subject to network behavior and wallet approval.

Cryptocurrency transfers may be irreversible. Always verify the recipient address and network before approving a payment. Availability may vary by wallet, network, asset, and jurisdiction.
